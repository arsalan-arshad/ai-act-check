## Read
- The category is **already crowded within a few months of the 2 Aug 2026 date**, and the closest competitor matches our planned design almost feature for feature, at low prices ($20/month to €29 one-time).
- The maker of Article 50 Ready reports that only 14 of 495 large EU sites had a detectable chat widget. If true, the addressable group with a visible chatbot is small. Unverified.
- Combined with spike C1 (chatbot disclosure and marking are provider duties), the case for a new standalone product is **weak**.

## Recommendation
Park or drop idea 03 as a standalone product. Not recommended: building a competing scanner. If the owner still wants an angle, the only untested one is selling a lawyer-reviewed audit as a service to agencies, and that is a services offer, not a product. Revisit only if services conversations ask for it.
